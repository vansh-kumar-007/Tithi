Privacy Policy & Google Calendar Permissions

How Tithi protects your birth data, Supabase account, and Google Calendar connection.

1. Minimal Google Calendar Permissions (OAuth Scopes)

Tithi requests only the minimum OAuth scopes strictly required to synchronize Hindu birthday reminders:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — Used solely to create, update, verify ownership of, and delete Tithi-created Hindu birthday events on your primary Google Calendar.
  • openid & email — Used solely to identify and display which Google account email is connected in your My Account dashboard.

Tithi NEVER requests permission to read Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, or unrelated personal calendar entries.

2. What Tithi Writes to Google Calendar

Each Hindu birthday is created as an individual all-day event (since Hindu lunar dates shift across Gregorian dates every year). Event descriptions include only the profile label, Lunar Month, Paksha & Tithi, local Tithi window, and observance convention.

Privacy Protection: Tithi deliberately omits sensitive birth details such as your exact date of birth, exact birth time, and birthplace address/coordinates from Google Calendar event descriptions.

3. Encrypted Token Storage & Non-Exposure

Google OAuth refresh tokens and access tokens are encrypted on the backend server using Fernet authenticated encryption (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) before being saved in an isolated server-only PostgreSQL table locked from browser access. Tokens, client secrets, and encryption keys are never sent to browser JavaScript or stored in localStorage.

4. Disconnecting, Removing Events & Account Deletion
  • You can click Disconnect Google Calendar at any time in My Account (both Free and Plus tiers, including after a trial expires). Tithi revokes its OAuth token with Google and deletes encrypted credentials immediately.
  • Tithi tags only the calendar events it creates and verifies both database ownership and Tithi's private event markers before deleting any event. Personal Google Calendar events are never touched.
  • Deleting your Tithi account permanently removes all saved profiles, calculation history, entitlements, and OAuth connections, while leaving external Google Calendar events untouched unless you explicitly delete them first.
